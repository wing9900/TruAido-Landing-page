Questions

Straight answers.

Do I sign a contract?
No. Service is month to month, cancel anytime. And your first 30 days are covered by a full money-back guarantee on top of that.
I already have a website.
No problem. Every client gets the full rebuild included: we pull over what's worth keeping from your current site, your photos, your content, your best reviews, build the new one on our system, and point your domain at it. We don't patch old code, so you get the same quality build as everyone else, and your domain stays registered in your name, always.
I'm not good with technology.
There's one simple app, and it works like texting. Leads show up as conversations, you reply the way you'd reply to any text. If you can text, you can run this. We handle all the setup and the technical side for you.
What do I still have to do?
Answer the conversations. The system gets the customer talking to you, keeps the reviews coming, and brings past customers back. It doesn't quote the job. Most owners spend a few minutes a day in the app replying to leads. If nobody on your end replies, no system fixes that.
What happens right after I pay?
You fill out a 10-minute onboarding form, we start the build, your missed-call text-back and reactivation campaign go live in week one, and the full website and the rest of your system follow in about 7 days.
Will this get me more leads, or just handle the ones I have?
Both, but not at the same speed. The fast part is capturing all of your current lead flow and reactivating dormant relationships: calls that go to voicemail, form fills nobody answered, past customers who haven't heard from you in a year. Reaching them doesn't depend on Google, so it starts in week one. The slower part is growing new lead volume, which depends on how satisfied your customers are and how quickly you move up in Google search. Most owners find the system is well worth it on their existing lead volume alone.
How fast will I actually see results?
Different pieces run on different clocks. Your missed-call text-back and reactivation campaign go live in week one, and both can book jobs immediately because they work with demand you already have. The past-customer review push starts then too, paced over several weeks. Ranking is the slow one: local SEO usually takes a few months to move, and longer in a competitive city. Anyone promising you page one in thirty days is guessing. The 90-day guarantee measures whether the system has paid for itself, not whether you've reached the top of Google Maps.
I'm a newer business without many past customers. Is this for me?
Yes. The website, missed-call text-back, review engine, and your Google Business Profile all work from day one. Two pieces lean on an existing customer list, the week-one reactivation campaign and the past-customer review push, so with a short list those start small and grow as you finish jobs. Everything else works the same, including both guarantees.
Does this include a CRM?
Yes. Contacts, conversation history, jobs, and appointments all live in one place, the same app your leads arrive in. Nothing extra to buy, nothing to sync.
I already use Jobber, Housecall Pro, or QuickBooks. Does this work with them?
Yes. Keep running your jobs in the tools you already like. Our system handles the front door, the leads, missed calls, reviews, and campaigns, and we connect the two so new customers flow into your existing setup. We map that out with you in onboarding.
Is this like HomeAdvisor or Angi leads?
No, and this matters. Those services sell the same lead to you and four other companies, and you pay whether it turns into a job or not. Everything this system produces comes from your own website, your own Google Business Profile, your own phone number, and your own customer list. Every lead is yours alone, and you never pay per lead.
If I cancel, what do I keep?
Your domain is always registered in your name and your customer list is always yours to take, no games. The website and automations run on our system while you're a client, which is how we can do this month to month with no contract instead of charging thousands up front. Nobody holds your business hostage here, in either direction.
What if it doesn't work in my market?
That's what the guarantee is for. 30 days risk-free for any reason, and if the system hasn't paid for itself by day 90, you don't pay again until it has. Every lead is logged in the system where we can both see it, so there's no arguing about what came from where.
Is this one of those AI bots that annoys customers?
No. The messages sound like you, you approve every one of them before anything goes live, and the moment a real conversation starts, it's you talking, not a bot.
